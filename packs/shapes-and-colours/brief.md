# Shapes & Colours -- brief

**Assumptions.** Young children (about ages 3-6) with a grown-up; English and Bulgarian, one
activity per language; text-first and silent (no eSpeak NG where it was drawn).

**Learning goals.** Name nine flat shapes (circle, triangle, square, rectangle, star, oval,
hexagon, rhombus, pentagon) by their sides, corners and points; name six colours; find a shape
in a row by a property (three corners, can roll) or by its colour.

**Not by colour alone.** Every shape has a dark outline, and every colour has its own pattern:
red solid, blue horizontal stripes, yellow dots, green slanted stripes, orange checks, purple
grid. A card that asks about a colour also names the shapes, and its reveal gives the shape,
position (left, middle, right), colour and pattern. Alternative text on a prompt face
describes each shape's outline and pattern without its count or name ("straight sides, all the
same length, like a honeycomb cell"), and the colour card lists its colours apart from the
shapes, so a screen-reader user answers the same question; the reveal face's text gives
everything, counts included.

**Order.** Nine single shapes (familiar -> less familiar), then three "find it in a row" cards:
three corners (right), purple (middle), which can roll (middle). Answer positions vary.

**Pictures.** Twelve 800 x 800 drawings in code (`draw.py`).

**Timing.** Prompt 7 s, recall 3 s, reveal 8 s, dwell 2 s: 20 s a card, 4 min in all.
