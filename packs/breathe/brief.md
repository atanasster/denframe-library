# Breathe -- brief

**Assumptions.** Anyone at home who wants a slow, even breathing pace on a shared screen,
including children with a grown-up. English only. No sound, no quiz.

**What it does.** One activity of six cycles in the 4-4-4-4 pattern often called box breathing,
which ends after the sixth (it does not loop -- "Six slow breaths" -- decided after review): breathe in for four, hold for four, breathe out for four, hold for four. The card's
own timing is the pace: prompt 4 s (in), recall 4 s (hold, the prompt still showing), reveal
4 s (out), dwell 4 s (hold, the reveal still showing) -- 16 s a cycle, about 96 s for six. The
pace is approximate: the player takes a moment between cards to load the next one. The first
card says to sit comfortably and to stop and breathe normally if dizzy; the last says to breathe
normally.

**No health claims.** The description says it is not medical advice and to stop if dizzy, and
the first card says so on the screen. The
pattern is described after Cleveland Clinic's *Box breathing* article; the pack claims no
benefit.

**Pictures.** Two 800 x 800 drawings in code (`draw.py`): five full rings to breathe in by, one
small ring with faint dotted rings to breathe out by, and four decorative dots around them
(they do not count; nothing on the card paces the holds but its timing). They are
still pictures: nothing animates, so reduced-motion viewers see exactly what everyone sees; the
only change is the card turning from prompt to reveal, as every activity does.

**Review.** The owner reviewed it on 28 September 2026 -- content, licence and design (every
picture) passed. No Bulgarian (fluent not applicable), no audio (listening not applicable).
